# Restyle the ZANKYO footer from the reference

## What will change

- Change only the footer; keep the header, pages, products, Shopify features, and all other website sections unchanged.
- Rebuild the footer in the reference’s compact streetwear-editorial style:
  - wide black footer band
  - ZANKYO logo anchored on the left
  - small uppercase column headings
  - tighter link rows and thin divider lines
  - social links integrated into a **Follow us** column instead of large pill buttons
  - copyright in a restrained bottom row
- Use a horizontal multi-column arrangement on laptops and a clean stacked arrangement on phones.

## Content that stays

- Existing ZANKYO kanji logo and tagline artwork.
- Shop links: All products, Collections, About.
- Support links: Return & Refund Policy, Terms & Conditions.
- Customer support email: help@zankyo.in.
- Existing clickable Instagram and WhatsApp Community destinations, still opening safely in a new tab.
- Existing copyright wording and automatic current year.

## Scope and verification

- Do not add the reference brand’s wording, newsletter form, categories, or imagery.
- Keep accessible labels and comfortable phone tap targets despite the more compact appearance.
- Verify the footer at phone and laptop sizes, including link visibility, alignment, no sideways scrolling, and no new page errors.
